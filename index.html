IN AN EMERGENCY

Stay Calm and Follow These Steps

01

Check Your Safety

Move away from immediate danger when possible. Do not put yourself at unnecessary risk.

02

Assess the Situation

Identify what happened, how many people are affected and whether there are immediate hazards.

03

Contact Emergency Services

For serious or life-threatening emergencies, call the appropriate emergency service and follow the operator's instructions.

04

Provide Basic Assistance

If it is safe to do so, provide appropriate first aid while waiting for professional help.
