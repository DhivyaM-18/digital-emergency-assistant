Emergency Safety Guide

Simple habits and preparation can help you respond better during an emergency.

QUICK GUIDANCE

Know What To Do

🚨

Stay Calm

Take a breath and assess the situation before taking action.

  • Avoid panic.
  • Look for immediate dangers.
  • Move to a safer location if necessary.
📞

Call for Help

Contact the appropriate emergency service when professional assistance is needed.

  • Explain what happened clearly.
  • Give your location.
  • Follow the operator's instructions.
🧰

Emergency Kit

Keep essential supplies ready so you can respond quickly.

  • Keep a first-aid kit available.
  • Keep a flashlight and batteries.
  • Keep drinking water available.
🏠

Home Safety

Make your home safer by identifying possible hazards.

  • Know emergency exits.
  • Keep important numbers accessible.
  • Avoid blocking escape routes.
🚗

Road Safety

Follow basic safety practices when travelling by road.

  • Wear a seat belt.
  • Follow traffic signals.
  • Avoid distracted driving.
🌧️

Severe Weather

Take precautions when severe weather conditions occur.

  • Follow official alerts.
  • Stay indoors when advised.
  • Avoid dangerous flooded areas.
