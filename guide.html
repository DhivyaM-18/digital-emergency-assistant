Emergency
Safety Guide

Practical safety information for common emergencies and disaster situations. Stay calm, move to a safe location and follow instructions from emergency authorities.

QUICK REFERENCE

Common Emergency Situations

Select a situation to learn the important steps to take and actions to avoid.

01

Fire Emergency

Leave the building quickly, use the safest available exit and never use an elevator during a fire.

  • Raise the alarm and call emergency services.
  • Stay low if there is smoke.
  • Never return to a burning building.
  • Stop, drop and roll if clothing catches fire.
02

Flood

Move to higher ground and stay away from rapidly moving or contaminated water.

  • Follow evacuation instructions.
  • Avoid walking through floodwater.
  • Never drive through flooded roads.
  • Stay away from fallen electrical wires.
03

Earthquake

Protect yourself from falling objects while the ground is shaking.

  • Drop, cover and hold on.
  • Stay away from windows and glass.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • After shaking stops, move carefully to a safe area.
04

Cyclone & Severe Storm

Stay indoors and monitor official warnings throughout the storm.

  • Secure doors and windows.
  • Stay away from windows during strong winds.
  • Keep emergency supplies nearby.
  • Follow evacuation orders from authorities.
05

Heatstroke

Heatstroke can become life-threatening quickly. Move the person to a cooler environment and seek medical assistance.

  • Move the person to a cool or shaded place.
  • Remove unnecessary outer clothing.
  • Cool the person with appropriate methods.
  • Seek emergency medical help immediately.
06

Gas Leak

If you suspect a gas leak, move away from the area and avoid anything that could create a spark.

  • Do not switch electrical appliances on or off.
  • Do not use matches, lighters or open flames.
  • Move outside to fresh air.
  • Contact the appropriate emergency service.
