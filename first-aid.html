FIRST AID INFORMATION

Learn. Respond. Stay Safe.

Simple first-aid information for common emergency situations.
⚠️

Important Safety Notice

First-aid information is provided for general guidance only. In a serious emergency, contact qualified medical professionals immediately.

🔥

Burns

Basic first-aid steps for minor burns caused by heat or hot objects.

What to do

  1. Move away from the source of heat.
  2. Cool the affected area with clean running water.
  3. Remove tight items near the affected area if possible.
  4. Cover the burn with a clean non-stick dressing.
Seek medical help for serious, deep or extensive burns.
🩹

Cuts & Wounds

Basic care for minor cuts, scratches and small wounds.

What to do

  1. Wash your hands before helping.
  2. Rinse the wound gently with clean water.
  3. Apply gentle pressure with clean gauze to control bleeding.
  4. Cover the wound with a clean dressing.
Get medical attention if bleeding does not stop or the wound is deep.
🦴

Fractures

Basic guidance for suspected broken bones or serious injuries.

What to do

  1. Keep the injured person as still as possible.
  2. Do not try to straighten the injured body part.
  3. Support the injured area without moving it unnecessarily.
  4. Contact emergency medical services.
Suspected fractures require professional medical evaluation.
🩸

Severe Bleeding

Basic actions to take when someone has significant external bleeding.

What to do

  1. Call emergency medical services.
  2. Apply firm, direct pressure using clean cloth or gauze.
  3. Keep pressure on the wound until help arrives.
  4. Keep the person as calm and still as possible.
Severe bleeding is a medical emergency. Get professional help immediately.
😵

Fainting

Basic steps for helping someone who has briefly lost consciousness.

What to do

  1. Check whether the person is breathing normally.
  2. Help them lie down safely.
  3. Loosen tight clothing around the neck.
  4. Allow them to recover calmly.
If the person does not regain consciousness or is not breathing normally, call emergency services.
🐝

Bites & Stings

Basic care for common insect bites and stings.

What to do

  1. Move away from the source of the bite or sting.
  2. Wash the affected area gently.
  3. Apply a cool compress to reduce swelling.
  4. Monitor for signs of a serious allergic reaction.
Seek emergency help if there is difficulty breathing or severe swelling.
